
# Yoshi Labs

> *Build deep. Ship real. Learn in public.*

Yoshi Labs is a technical space based in **Douala, Cameroon** — part personal lab, part open community for developers who care about how things actually work under the hood.

We build software with intention: systems that are fast, observable, and well-understood — not just functional.

---

## What this is

Two things at once:

- **A personal engineering lab** — where I (Yoshi) document my journey toward backend and systems engineering through real projects, not tutorials.
- **A growing community** — for developers in Cameroon and beyond who want to go beyond surface-level tech and understand the internals.

---

## Philosophy

- **Understand before you use.** We don't just reach for abstractions — we understand what's underneath them.
- **Build in public.** Rough edges and all. Learning documented honestly is more valuable than polished silence.
- **Performance is a feature.** Constrained environments teach you what matters. We take hardware limits seriously.
- **Depth over hype.** No chasing trends. Systems, compilers, networks, security — the foundations that don't expire.

## Projects in progress

### YAOT — Yet Another One Transpiler
A JavaScript-to-bytecode compiler written in Rust.  
Goal: understand lexing, parsing, AST construction, and code generation from scratch.  
Status: *in design phase*

### VPS Infrastructure
A clean, secure, production-grade self-hosted setup on a Ubuntu 24.04 VPS.  
Covers: Nginx reverse proxy, Docker, Pterodactyl, hardened Linux, custom domain.  
Status: *in progress*

### Systems Internals Series
A collection of write-ups and small projects covering:
- Linux internals (systemd, cgroups, namespaces)
- PostgreSQL and Redis under load
- Networking from sockets to HTTP
- Observability and profiling
